I am a PhD candidate at Harbin Institute of Technology, currently a visiting PhD student at the University of Auckland funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). I work on offshore hydrodynamics, using theoretical analysis, numerical modelling and wave-flume experiments to study the interaction of waves with structures, currents and the seabed. My recent work focuses on identifying wave loads from observations of the water surface, and on quantifying the uncertainty when wave and flow fields are reconstructed from surface data. I am glad to hear from anyone working on structural hydrodynamics, field monitoring or inverse problems.
