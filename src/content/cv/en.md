## Research experience

### Wave and flow field reconstruction under uncertainty · 2024–present

Research on inferring wave and flow fields from free-surface observations, using probabilistic and Bayesian methods to describe uncertainty in reconstructed quantities.

### Wave load identification for fixed and floating structures · 2022–2024

Research on physics-based mappings from observed wave elevation to structural loads. The studies consider fixed structures with arbitrary cross-sections and floating cylinders, with the latter using both free-surface and structural motion measurements.

### Wave load reduction for monopile-supported offshore wind turbines · 2021–2022

Analytical, numerical, and experimental work on the hydrodynamic effects of attached plates. The research combines potential-flow models and eigenfunction expansions with boundary-element calculations and model tests.
